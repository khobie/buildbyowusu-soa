Our History

Ridge School of Anaesthesia was established in 2008 as a specialist training institution within the Ridge Medical Centre campus. We offer a single flagship qualification: the BSc in Anaesthesia — a four-year programme combining academic excellence with immersive clinical training.

Over 1,200 graduates have passed through our doors, now serving in hospitals across Ghana and West Africa.

Mission

To train competent, ethical anaesthesia practitioners through excellence in education and clinical practice.

Vision

To be West Africa's leading centre of excellence in anaesthesia education and research.
