Who We Are

About the School

Ridge School of Anaesthesia is a premier medical training institution dedicated to producing competent, compassionate anaesthesia practitioners.

Founded in 2008, we combine rigorous academic curricula with hands-on clinical experience across Ghana's leading hospitals. Our graduates serve in theatres, ICUs, and critical care units nationwide.
